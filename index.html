Behali Wildlife Sanctuary: 157.6 km² of forest in Biswanath, Assam

Semi-evergreen forest in the eastern foothills of the Himalayas, between Nameri and Kaziranga national parks. Home to elephants, hornbills and clouded leopards.

How to reach

By air

Nearest airports: Donyi Polo Airport, Hollongi, Itanagar (IATA: HGI, ICAO: VEHO), and Tezpur Airport (IATA: TEZ, ICAO: VETZ), also called Salonibari Air Force Station. Tezpur is mostly reserved for the Indian Air Force, and civil flights are mostly suspended for security reasons.

By rail

Nearest railway station: Niz Bargang Railway Station (code: NBX).

By road

The sanctuary is about 30 km from Biswanath Chariali. There is no bus station near the sanctuary, so travel by road in a car, taxi or local transport from Biswanath Chariali.
