FAQ and fees

Can I book a jeep safari online?

Not at the moment. Jeep safari booking is not available for the time being. This page will carry the announcement when it opens.

How will online booking work?

Visitors will register with a mobile number and a one-time password, choose a date and time slot, and pay online. Booking rules and limits will be published here before it opens.

Where do I get help with a booking?

Contact the Range Forest Office. Details are on the Contact page.
